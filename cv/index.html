Professional Summary

FNRS Chargée de Recherche at LISA, Université libre de Bruxelles. Research on plenoptic camera systems, depth image-based rendering, and free-viewpoint navigation. Lead developer of the open-source Reference View Synthesis (RVS) software, adopted as the MPEG-I view synthesis reference tool; active contributor to MPEG Immersive Video standardization since 2017.
