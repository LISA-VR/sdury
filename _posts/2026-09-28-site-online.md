This site is the new home for my research at the LISA laboratory of the Université libre de Bruxelles. It brings together my publications (with open-access PDFs and citation counts, kept in sync automatically from Google Scholar), the open-source software I maintain, and a short description of the direction the group's view-synthesis work is heading.

If you are interested in depth image-based rendering, non-Lambertian scene representation, plenoptic camera systems, or immersive-video standardization, the [publications]({{ site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}/publications/) and [about]({{ site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}/about/) pages are the place to start.
