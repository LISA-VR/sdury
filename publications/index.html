2021

  1. Real-Time Depth Video-Based Rendering for 6-DoF HMD Navigation and Light Field Displays
    D. Bonatto, S. Fachada, S. Rogge, and 2 more authors
    In IEEE Access, 2021
    IEEE Access 9, 146868-146887
    Abs DOI

    This paper presents a novel approach to provide immersive free navigation with 6 Degrees of Freedom in real-time for natural and virtual scenery, for both static and dynamic content. Stemming from the state of the art in Depth Image-Based Rendering and the OpenGL pipeline, this new View Synthesis method achieves free navigation at up to 90 FPS and can take any number of input views with their corresponding depth maps as priors. Our results are comparable, objectively and subjectively, to the state of the art view synthesis tools NeRF and LLFF, while maintaining a lower complexity and real-time rendering.
